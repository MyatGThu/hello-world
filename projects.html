The Proof.

Three case files and a standing lab. The pattern in all of them: find what actually broke, fix it once, and write it down so the next person is faster.

Case files

A few problems I've owned from ticket to resolution.

  1. CASE_01Endpoint · Rollout

    Store-fleet dock standardisation

    Killed recurring dock failures across 400+ stores with a simpler, documented setup.

    Open case file →

    Retail laptop docks were failing constantly and costing money to replace right across the store estate.

    Objective
    Stop the steady stream of failing, costly laptop docks across 400+ retail stores.
    My role
    Replaced the Lenovo docks with daisy-chained hub monitors and wrote the rollout playbook for the service desk.
    Outcome
    Simpler desk setups, far fewer dock failures, and a repeatable process anyone on the team could run.

    • Documentation
  2. CASE_02ITSM · Knowledge

    Service-desk playbook

    Turned tribal knowledge and repeat tickets into a documented, repeatable process.

    Open case file →

    The desk leaned on a few people's memory; the same issues kept coming back and new starters took a while to get up to speed.

    Objective
    Turn tribal knowledge and repeat tickets into a consistent, documented process.
    My role
    Documented SOPs, KBAs and escalation paths, and became the second-level point the desk leans on.
    Outcome
    Faster, more consistent resolutions and a knowledge base new starters could pick up quickly.
    • ITSM
    • KBAs
    • Process
  3. CASE_03Identity · Devices

    Modern device builds & access

    Got staff onto secure, standardised devices with the right access from day one.

    Open case file →

    Device builds and access were manual and inconsistent, creating rework and the odd security gap.

    Objective
    Get staff onto secure, standardised devices with the right access from day one.
    My role
    Built laptops to the SOE, handled Active Directory and M365 administration, and ran joiner/leaver processes end to end.
    Outcome
    Consistent, compliant endpoints and a tidy account lifecycle with less manual rework.

    • Active Directory
